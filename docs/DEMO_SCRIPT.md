# Demo script for Constant Media (about 15 minutes)

Before the call: run `/selftest/` on the live link, sign in once, and keep a
second tab open on the Network console. Use a headset or a quiet room.

1. **The link (1 min).** Sign in with the access code and show the four views.
   Point out the "Simulated data" strip on the console and report.
2. **Schedule and the first ad slot (2 min).** Doctor view: today's schedule with
   one sponsored card beside it. Open *Why am I seeing this?*: specialty and
   state only, never anything said in a visit. Change the practice profile from
   Family medicine to Cardiology and the card changes.
3. **Pre-visit summary (1 min).** Open a patient: problems, medicines, allergies,
   eGFR, care gaps. The ad has gone: nothing sponsored on screens with patient data.
4. **Live visit (4 min).** Start recording, log consent, role-play a short visit
   with a medicine change, a dose and an allergy. The live transcript follows
   along; the patient's medicines are listed as key terms. End the visit.
5. **The wait (30 s).** The final pass, note, verification and medicine checks
   run, with the note-drafting sponsored card on a screen that shows no patient
   data. It disappears the moment the draft is ready.
6. **Review (4 min).** Click evidence chips to show the exact words behind each
   line. Show a line the checker flagged. Confirm medicines; show the sound-alike
   picker and a high-risk dose confirmation. Open the safety alerts: an
   interaction with its FDA label source, a kidney-function flag, the formulary
   tier. Acknowledge a critical alert with a reason, then sign.
7. **Note complete (30 s).** The post-sign card, for the clinician only.
8. **Network console (2 min).** The impressions from steps 2, 5 and 7 appear
   within seconds as test traffic, kept out of billable totals. Walk campaign
   set-up, the ad-load setting with sellable inventory, delivery and pacing, and
   the verification panel with the event-log fingerprint.
9. **Sponsor report (1 min).** Open a read-only share link: delivered vs booked,
   viewable impressions, prescribers reached, frequency, CPM and spend.

Safety net: if the microphone or network misbehaves, use *Replay a scripted
visit* on the patient's chart; it runs the same pipeline on a prepared transcript.

Never say "accurate to X%" on the call: we have not run an independent
evaluation yet. Say what the product does to catch errors (evidence links,
line-by-line verification, every medicine confirmed by the doctor).
